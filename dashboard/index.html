Why this is not a KeeperHub wrapper
Renn decides.
KeeperHub executes.
Renn proves.

Execution transport is KeeperHub's job and it is load-bearing. The decision state machine, the obligation, and the independent proof are Renn's. Neither can stand in for the other.

RENN DECIDES decision state machine

  • finality
  • obligation
  • beneficiary
  • amount
  • dependency
  • proof

KEEPERHUB EXECUTES sponsored execution

  • simulation
  • broadcast
  • gas
  • transaction

RENN PROVES independent verification

  • finality
  • exact transfer
  • beneficiary
  • amount
  • predecessor proof
Renn never signs and never holds keys. The money logic is a normal, inspectable KeeperHub workflow — but KeeperHub's receipt alone never closes an obligation. Renn re-reads the chain itself and only a verified exact transfer becomes the proof that unlocks the next payment.
